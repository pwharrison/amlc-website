## Why Sponsor?

The AMLC brings together hundreds of leading data scientists, AI engineers, computational researchers, and other technical leaders from across the country, spanning both industry and academia, and ranging from individual contributors to VPs and C-level executives. Partnering with the AMLC gives your organization **direct access to this unusually concentrated group** of decision-makers and technical talent, whether you're recruiting, showcasing a product or platform, demonstrating your organization's thought leadership, or building brand visibility.

**Who attends:**

- **58% industry, 32% academic.** Reach industry practitioners and academic researchers and educators at the same event.
- **27% managers or executives**, and 1 in 9 is a founder, CEO, or other C-level executive.
- **75%+ hold a graduate degree.** One in four holds a PhD.
- **Participants from 35 cities representing 20 states and provinces** across the US and Canada.

## Sponsorship Packages

We offer sponsorship packages at four levels — Diamond, Platinum, Gold, and Silver — with benefits including expo booths, the chance to present a talk or workshop, premium logo placement, job board listings, included conference passes, and more.

A sponsorship of the conference's official social and networking event on Friday evening is also available, and is limited to one organization.

Need something else? We're happy to explore a custom package that fits your goals.
